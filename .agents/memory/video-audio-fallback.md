---
name: Video audio fallback
description: Audio generation may be unavailable during video builds, so preserve the timed narration script and use a stable instrumental fallback rather than blocking the visual deliverable.
---

When narration or generative music services are unavailable, keep the requested narration copy synchronized in the scenes, use a deterministic instrumental fallback if needed, and defer only the missing rendered voice track.

**Why:** The commercial can still be reviewed for visual timing, factual claims, and edit rhythm without substituting a low-quality synthetic narrator or leaving the export without an audio bed.

**How to apply:** Treat the narrator as a separate follow-up deliverable, and preserve the existing scene timing when adding it later.
